TransProxy adapts the actual CSS view-timeline, grow-y spine, perspective
hinge and conic --sweep border mechanisms to existing planned-feature
previews. Selectors/keyframes are scoped, colors stay blue/pink, hinge
angles are restrained, and every new animation respects reduced motion.
Feature copy remains stable. Original demo text, typography, full-screen
spacing, backdrop, blur effects, year counter and scripts are not imported.
