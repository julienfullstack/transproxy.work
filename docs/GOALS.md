## Planned product goals

1. **Preserve a patient's understanding before care.** Save exact versions of wishes, explanations, questions, agreements and refusals, and compare these with later records and experience.
2. **Make recovery documentation useful.** Link appointments, photos, healing stages, fluid/drain logs and care contacts without automatically sharing them.
3. **Keep each disclosure deliberate.** Select exact fields and attachments for private recipients, publication, gallery contributions or pattern opt-ins; support export, withdrawal and revocation.
4. **Expose patterns with context.** Keep source types and provider responses distinct, review identity matching, deduplicate incidents and suppress revealing small groups.
5. **Support respectful community exchange.** Invite-only adult participation, anonymous public attribution choices, guided feedback, bounded volunteer roles and separately invited groups.
6. **Make reliable help easier to find.** Source-backed provider, advocacy and legal directories, plus reviewed healthcare and procedure references.

## Member-product acceptance evidence, before claiming availability

- Two-account and role tests prove ownership, selected-field sharing and subgroup boundaries; blocked or revoked sessions cannot continue access.
- End-to-end content and keys remain absent from server plaintext, logs and Stream requests during private use. Recovery behavior matches the chosen mode; password reset cannot silently weaken it.
- Exact record versions, event/entry timestamps, concurrent edits and interrupted uploads preserve information without silent replacement.
- Share invitations/links, recipient checks, expiry, revocation and selected exports behave as described.
- Public endpoints expose only the approved snapshot; originals, private notes, metadata and contact details stay private. Editing published material re-enters review.
- Stream policy readback and image tests confirm gore is allowed, incomplete screening remains pending, and Cruush configuration is unchanged.
- Pattern checks cover duplicate incidents, ambiguous identities, small cohorts, filter combinations, withdrawal and private-data exclusion.
- Directory entries carry primary sources and last-checked dates. Healthcare references identify edition, revisions and actual review status rather than claiming review that did not occur.
- Adults-only access, invitation requirements and public/member/private visibility work in representative states. Jurisdiction-specific launch requirements are reviewed before expanding applicable services.
- Backup restore, key recovery/rotation, deletion/retention, operational access and real desktop/mobile HTTPS flows are verified before corresponding promises are presented as implemented.

Each milestone records local, committed, pushed, deployed and live-verified status separately. A passing build or design screenshot alone does not prove a production flow.
